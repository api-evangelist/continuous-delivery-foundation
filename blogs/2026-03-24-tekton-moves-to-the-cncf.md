<div class="wpb_text_column wpb_content_element ">
	<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">One of the CD Foundation’s founding and graduated projects, </span><a href="https://tekton.dev/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Tekton</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, is in the process of moving to the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF), which is also part of The Linux Foundation.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">&#8220;The CNCF Technical Oversight Committee (TOC) has voted to accept Tekton as a CNCF incubating project.&#8221; Read the</span><a href="https://www.cncf.io/blog/2026/03/24/tekton-becomes-a-cncf-incubating-project/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"> <span style="font-weight: 400;">CNCF announcement</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>

<div class="wpb_text_column wpb_content_element ">
	<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Tekton&#8217;s journey at the CDF</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The CD Foundation was created in 2019 around four founding projects: Jenkins, Jenkins X, Spinnaker, and Tekton. Spun out of the Knative Build component, Tekton was part of the CDF&#8217;s DNA from day one — and the foundation provided the neutral ground for it to grow into the project it is today.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">During its time at the CDF, Tekton matured significantly:</span></p>
<ul>
<li>Reaching its <b>stable v1.0 release</b></li>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Building a </span><b>multi-vendor governance model</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with contributors from Red Hat, Google, IBM, and many individual contributors</span></li>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Establishing </span><b>supply chain security</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> through Tekton Chains, bringing signing and attestation to CI/CD pipelines</span></li>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Growing to </span><b>600+ contributors</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><b>11,000+ GitHub stars</b></li>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Becoming the backbone of commercial offerings like Red Hat OpenShift Pipelines and IBM Cloud Continuous Delivery</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Tekton became one of the </span><a href="https://cd.foundation/blog/2022/11/10/tekton-graduated-%f0%9f%8e%93/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CDF&#8217;s first Graduated projects</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> — a milestone that reflects the technical maturity and governance health the project achieved under the foundation&#8217;s stewardship.</span></p>

	<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">CDF&#8217;s Role in Tekton&#8217;s Maturity</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The CDF provided more than a neutral home — it gave Tekton a community. Through events like</span><a href="https://cd.foundation/cdcon-2026/"><span style="font-weight: 400;"> cdCon</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, Tekton maintainers and users connected, shared ideas, and shaped the project&#8217;s direction together. Collaboration with sibling CDF projects was equally formative: Tekton was instrumental in creating </span><a href="https://cdevents.dev/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CDEvents</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, bringing interoperability to CI/CD pipelines, and </span><a href="https://shipwright.io/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Shipwright</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> chose Tekton as its foundation for container image builds.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In many ways, Tekton graduated</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> from</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> the CDF — it arrived as a young project and leaves as a mature, battle-tested framework that helped define what Kubernetes-native CI/CD looks like for the industry.</span></p>

	<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">What does this mean for Tekton users?</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nothing changes for users. Tekton remains the same open source project with the same codebase, the same maintainers, and the same commitment to stability and backwards compatibility. The governance structure carries over, and all existing integrations, distributions, and tooling continue to work exactly as before.</span></p>

The security audit the foundation sponsored in 2022 is one example — a concrete commitment to the project&#8217;s health that went well beyond providing a neutral home. The people we met, the community that grew around us, the conversations at cdCon — those stay with us. This transition is bittersweet; we&#8217;re grateful for everything the CDF has done for Tekton, and we look forward to continuing to work together across the two foundations.&#8221;</span></p>

<div class="wpb_text_column wpb_content_element ">
	<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Not Goodbye, Just New Neighbors</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As Andrea Frittoli noted when Shipwright made a similar move: &#8220;This isn&#8217;t truly goodbye — CNCF and CDF are open source community neighbors, there&#8217;s plenty of opportunity for collaboration.&#8221;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The CDF and CNCF are both part of The Linux Foundation family, and the two foundations continue to collaborate on shared goals around continuous delivery, software supply chain security, and developer experience. CDF projects like </span><a href="https://cdevents.dev"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CDEvents</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> — which Tekton was instrumental in creating — continue to bridge both communities.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">We celebrate Tekton&#8217;s journey and are proud of the role the CDF played in its growth. We wish the Tekton community all the best in their new home.</span></p>
