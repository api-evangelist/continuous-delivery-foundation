	<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Let me start somewhere familiar. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">There’s a song: “</span><a href="https://youtu.be/ojULkWEUsPs" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Who Let the Dogs Out?</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">” It’s funny because it captures a feeling we all recognize: something got loose… and now everyone is reacting.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">But two components make up this scenario:</span></p>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The dogs didn’t get out on their own</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We opened the gate</span></li>
</ol>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Most of us have had a puppy, or at least know what that’s like. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">You don’t bring a puppy home and say:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Let’s see if it bites</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Let’s see if it chews the furniture</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Let’s see if it runs into the street</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You know it will, so you train it. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">You set boundaries. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">You shape behavior early, because you know something simple: Behavior compounds. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">And if that puppy runs into the street chasing a squirrel and gets hurt, we don’t stand there and say: “That was a bad dog.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">We say:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Where was the owner?</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Why wasn’t there a leash?</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Because we understand something instinctively; it’s not about blame, it’s about responsibility.</span></p>
</div>

	<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI Puppies Running Loose</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Now, let’s talk about AI.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Right now, we are in the “puppy phase” of AI.</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">It’s learning</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">It’s adapting</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">It’s responding to signals we don’t fully understand</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">And what are we doing?</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">We’re releasing capability first…and thinking about constraints later. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">We’re letting it run…and hoping it behaves.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">I hope this sounds familiar because it’s happened before with cybersecurity.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">We built systems with:</span></p>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hardcoded credentials</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Static identities </span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Flat networks</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Implicit trust</span></li>
</ol>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">And we said: </span><span style="color: #3366ff;"><b>We’ll secure it later.</b></span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Well, later came…at scale. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">And those early decisions didn’t stay small, they propagated like gremlins, (or IoT devices), and became systemic risk.</span></p>
</div>

<div class="wpb_text_column wpb_content_element ">
	<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Secure AI Now</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">When we talk about “securing AI,” I want to be very clear: </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">This is not about putting a leash on AI after the fact.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Because that’s not how complex systems work. You don’t chase behavior once it’s already running loose. You design the conditions that shape behavior before it ever emerges.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">So yes!</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Put the dog on the leash.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">But understand what that really means in our world:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Identity boundaries</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Data provenance</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Model transparency</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Governance that is built in…not bolted on</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You design them right from the start…secure by design.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This isn’t about blaming the builders, the researchers, or the community. It’s about ownership. We built this. We are shaping it…right now. And the conditions we set today will define the behavior we live with tomorrow.</span></p>
<p><b>Maybe the better question isn’t:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Who let the dogs out?</span></p>
<p><b>Maybe the question is: </b></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Are we willing to take responsibility for the environment we’ve created…before it’s too late to shape it?</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That’s the moment we’re in. Not fear. Not panic, but ownership. What a brilliant idea.</span></p>

	<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Join the effort to shape secure AI before the gate is fully open.</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The next chapter of software security is being written right now, and it’s not happening inside a single company or tool. It’s happening in the open. The </span><a href="https://cd.foundation/cybersecurity/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>CI/CD Cybersecurity SIG</b></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> under the Continuous Delivery Foundation is bringing together practitioners, researchers, and platform engineers to define what </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">secure-by-design AI in delivery pipelines</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> actually looks like in practice.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">We’re launching a new </span><b>AI Security chapter</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> in the CI/CD Cybersecurity Guide, and we’re looking for contributors who want to help answer the hard questions:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">How do we establish identity boundaries for agents?</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">What does provenance look like for models and prompts?</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Where should policy live in agent-enabled pipelines?</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">How do we prevent “secure later” from becoming our next legacy problem?</span></li>
</ul>
