We’re happy to announce the new Ambassador Chair for 2026/2027: Yair Etziony! The CDF Community Ambassador Program is a network of passionate community members, Continuous Delivery (CD) industry representatives, thought leaders, and local event organizers. These volunteers are committed to empowering our global community with skills, education, and support in CI/CD & DevOps. Yair was elected because he has been an exceptional Ambassador.
