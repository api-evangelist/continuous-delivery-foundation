This post discusses how organizations can measure the return on investment from AI adoption in software development. Using the "J-curve" metaphor, the article explains that AI initially causes a performance dip before delivering benefits, and that success requires strong foundational practices in organizational culture and continuous delivery before implementing AI tools.
